Questions

FAQ

Do credits expire?
No — your credits never expire. Use them at your own pace, whenever you feel ready.
Can I use credits for any service?
Yes. Credits apply to all services — body massage, bamboo facial therapy, and K-contour facial. 1 credit equals $1 CAD.
How do I use credits when I book?
Just let Kaylee know at the time of your appointment that you'd like to pay with credits. Credits are deducted from your account after the session.
Can I check my credit balance?
Yes — sign in to your account to view your current balance and full transaction history anytime.
What payment methods are accepted?
All major credit and debit cards are accepted through Stripe (Visa, Mastercard, Amex). Your payment information is securely handled by Stripe.
Can I purchase more than one package?
Yes. You can purchase as many packages as you'd like — credits accumulate in your account and never expire.
Can I get a refund on my credits?
Credit purchases are non-refundable. Credits may be transferred to an immediate family member upon request.
